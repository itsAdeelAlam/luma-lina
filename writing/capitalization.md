# Capitalization

**Status:** Official

## Purpose

This document defines the capitalization rules for written **Luma Lina**.

Capitalization is a writing convention. It does not change the grammatical function or meaning of a word.

---

## Sentence Beginnings

The first word of a sentence is capitalized.

Examples:

```text
Ei yum buff.
Ao la [teacher].
Ta maimai di la Ei di de maimai di.
```

Only the first word is capitalized because it begins the sentence.

---

## Personal Pronouns

Luma Lina personal pronouns are written with an initial capital letter when they appear as independent words.

Official pronouns:

* `Ei`
* `Ao`
* `Sa`
* `Nu`

Examples:

```text
Ei yum buff.
Ao la [teacher].
Sa na yum buff.
Nu la [animal].
```

When a pronoun is followed by `di`, the pronoun remains capitalized:

```text
Ei di
Ao di
Sa di
Nu di
```

---

## Grammatical Markers

Grammatical markers are normally written in lowercase.

Examples:

```text
di
de
la
na
ma
```

They remain lowercase even when they appear in a sentence.

Examples:

```text
Ei di yum buff.
Ei de maimai.
Ei na yum buff.
Ei yum buff ma?
```

---

## Common Vocabulary

Common Luma Lina vocabulary is normally written in lowercase unless another capitalization rule applies.

Examples:

```text
yum
buff
maimai
luma
lina
```

Example sentence:

```text
Ei yum buff.
```

---

## Demonstratives

The demonstratives `ti` and `ta` are normally written in lowercase when they occur within a sentence.

Examples:

```text
ti maimai
ta maimai
ti maimai di
ta maimai di
```

At the beginning of a sentence, the normal sentence-capitalization rule applies.

Example:

```text
Ta maimai la [animal].
```

Here, `Ta` is capitalized because it is the first word of the sentence.

---

## Names and Proper Nouns

Names and other proper nouns are capitalized.

Examples:

```text
Adeel
```

Example:

```text
Ei la Adeel.
```

Proper nouns should retain their established capitalization when used in Luma Lina.

---

## The Language Name

The official name of the language is written as:

**Luma Lina**

Both words are capitalized because the name is a proper name.

---

## Punctuation and Capitalization

Capitalization does not change because of grammatical markers.

A sentence remains capitalized according to its position, not according to the grammatical role of a word.

Examples:

```text
Ei yum buff.
Ei yum buff ma?
Ei yum Sao?
Sao yum buff?
```

The first word is capitalized, while the other words follow their normal capitalization rules.

---

## Core Rules

1. The first word of a sentence is capitalized.
2. Personal pronouns are normally capitalized: `Ei`, `Ao`, `Sa`, `Nu`.
3. Grammatical markers are normally lowercase.
4. Common vocabulary is normally lowercase.
5. Demonstratives are normally lowercase within sentences.
6. Proper nouns are capitalized.
7. The language name is written **Luma Lina**.
8. Capitalization is a writing convention and does not change grammatical meaning.
