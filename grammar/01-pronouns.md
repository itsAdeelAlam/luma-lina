# Pronouns

**Status:** Official

## Overview

Luma Lina uses a simple pronoun system.

Pronouns do not change according to:

* person
* number
* gender

Plurality is expressed separately with the plural marker `di`.

The same pronoun therefore keeps the same form in different grammatical situations.

---

## Personal Pronouns

| Luma Lina | Meaning  |
| --------- | -------- |
| `Ei`      | I / me   |
| `Ao`      | you      |
| `Sa`      | he / she |
| `Nu`      | it       |

### `Ei`

`Ei` refers to the speaker.

```text
Ei yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** I eat meat.

---

### `Ao`

`Ao` refers to the person or people being addressed.

```text
Ao yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** You eat meat.

---

### `Sa`

`Sa` refers to a person other than the speaker or listener.

`Sa` does not distinguish grammatical gender.

It can mean either **he** or **she**, depending on context.

```text
Sa yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** He/she eats meat.

---

### `Nu`

`Nu` refers to a non-human or non-person entity.

```text
Nu la [animal].
```

**Meaning:** It is an animal.

---

## Plural Pronouns

Luma Lina does not create separate plural forms of pronouns.

The existing plural marker `di` is placed after the pronoun.

### `Ei di`

`Ei di` means **we / us**.

```text
Ei di yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** We eat meat.

### `Ao di`

`Ao di` means **you all**.

```text
Ao di yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** You all eat meat.

### `Sa di`

`Sa di` means **they** when referring to multiple people.

```text
Sa di yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** They eat meat.

### `Nu di`

`Nu di` means **they / those things** when referring to multiple non-person entities.

```text
Nu di la [animal].
```

**Meaning:** They are animals.

---

## Pluralization Rule

The plural marker `di` follows the word it pluralizes.

```text
Ei di

Ao di

Sa di

Nu di
```

This is the same general pluralization system used with nouns.

For example:

```text
maimai di
```

means **goats**.

The pronoun system therefore does not require separate words for `we`, `you all`, or `they`.

---

## Possessive Use

Pronouns can combine with the possession/relationship marker `de`.

The pronoun is pluralized first when necessary, and `de` follows the pronoun.

Examples:

```text
Ei de
```

**Meaning:** my

```text
Ei di de
```

**Meaning:** our

```text
Ao de
```

**Meaning:** your

```text
Sa de
```

**Meaning:** his / her

```text
Nu de
```

**Meaning:** its

The same compositional structure is used with plural pronouns.

```text
Ei di de maimai di
```

**Meaning:** our goats

Structure:

```text
Ei di + de + maimai di
we/us + possession + goats
```

---

## Gender

Luma Lina has no grammatical gender in its pronoun system.

`Sa` can refer to either a male or female person.

The form does not change based on gender.

```text
Sa la [teacher].
```

can mean:

* He is a teacher.
* She is a teacher.

The intended meaning is determined by context.

---

## Summary

| Form    | Meaning             |
| ------- | ------------------- |
| `Ei`    | I / me              |
| `Ao`    | you                 |
| `Sa`    | he / she            |
| `Nu`    | it                  |
| `Ei di` | we / us             |
| `Ao di` | you all             |
| `Sa di` | they                |
| `Nu di` | they / those things |

### Core Rules

1. Pronouns do not change for grammatical gender.
2. Pronouns do not have separate built-in plural forms.
3. `di` is used to express plurality.
4. `di` follows the pronoun it pluralizes.
5. `de` can follow a pronoun to express possession or relationship.
6. Pronouns do not cause verb conjugation.
