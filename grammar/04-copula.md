# Copula

**Status:** Official

## Overview

Luma Lina uses the word:

```text
la
```

`la` is the invariant copula. It expresses meanings such as:

* am
* is
* are

The form does not change according to person, number, or gender.

---

## Basic Rule

The basic copular structure is:

```text
Subject + la + Complement
```

Examples:

```text
Ei la Adeel.
```

= I am Adeel.

```text
Sa la [teacher].
```

= He / she is a teacher.

```text
Nu la [animal].
```

= It is an animal.

The copula `la` remains unchanged in every example.

---

## Plural Subjects

Plural subjects use the existing plural marker `di`.

The copula itself does not change.

```text
Ei di la [students].
```

= We are students.

```text
Sa di la [teachers].
```

= They are teachers.

The structure remains:

```text
Plural Subject + la + Complement
```

There is no separate plural form of `la`.

---

## Plural Complements

The complement can also contain a plural noun.

```text
Sa di la [teacher] di.
```

= They are teachers.

The plural marker `di` continues to follow the word it pluralizes.

When both the subject and complement are plural:

```text
Sa di la [teacher] di.
```

= They are teachers.

---

## Possession and the Copula

The possession marker `de` can appear inside the complement of a copular sentence.

For example:

```text
ta di la ei de maimai di
```

= Those are my goats.

Structure:

```text
ta di

those

la

copula

ei de maimai di

my goats
```

The possessive phrase follows the same possession structure already established:

```text
Ei de maimai di
```

= my goats

The copula `la` connects the subject with the possessive phrase.

Another established example is:

```text
ta di la ei di de maimai di
```

= Those are our goats.

Structure:

```text
ta di

those

la

copula

ei di de maimai di

our goats
```

This shows that the copula can be used with a possessive phrase containing both `de` and the plural marker `di`.

The existing rules remain unchanged:

* `la` marks the copular relationship.
* `de` marks possession or a relationship.
* `di` marks plurality.
* Each `di` follows the word it pluralizes.

---

## Demonstratives and the Copula

Demonstratives can function as subjects or complements.

For example:

```text
ta di la ei di de maimai di
```

= Those are our goats.

Structure:

```text
ta di

those

la

copula

ei di de maimai di

our goats
```

The copula remains `la` even though the subject is plural.

---

## Negation

Copular sentences use the existing general negation marker `na`.

The structure is:

```text
Subject + na + la + Complement
```

Example:

```text
Ei na la Adeel.
```

= I am not Adeel.

Another example:

```text
Sa na la [teacher].
```

= He / she is not a teacher.

`na` does not replace `la`. It comes before the predicate, which includes the copula.

---

## Questions

The existing question system can be used with copular sentences.

The yes/no question marker `ma` can be added to form a yes/no question.

```text
Ei la Adeel ma?
```

= Am I Adeel?

The copula remains unchanged.

Information questions use the established `Sao` system when a missing element needs to be questioned.

The copula itself does not change for questions.

---

## No Agreement on `la`

`la` does not change for:

* person
* number
* gender

The same form is used with all subjects.

| Subject | Copula | Example                |
| ------- | ------ | ---------------------- |
| `Ei`    | `la`   | `Ei la Adeel.`         |
| `Ao`    | `la`   | `Ao la [teacher].`     |
| `Sa`    | `la`   | `Sa la [teacher].`     |
| `Nu`    | `la`   | `Nu la [animal].`      |
| `Ei di` | `la`   | `Ei di la [students].` |
| `Sa di` | `la`   | `Sa di la [teachers].` |

---

## Core Rule

The official copular structure is:

> **Subject + `la` + Complement**

`la` is invariant and does not change according to the subject.

For negative copular sentences:

> **Subject + `na` + `la` + Complement**

For yes/no questions, the existing `ma` marker can be used without changing `la`.

---

## Summary

| Structure                   | Example                       | Meaning              |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Subject + `la` + Complement | `Ei la Adeel.`                | I am Adeel.          |
| Plural subject + `la`       | `Sa di la [teachers].`        | They are teachers.   |
| Negative copula             | `Ei na la Adeel.`             | I am not Adeel.      |
| Yes/no question             | `Ei la Adeel ma?`             | Am I Adeel?          |
| Demonstrative subject       | `ta di la ei di de maimai di` | Those are our goats. |

**Official rule:** `la` is the invariant copula used in the structure **Subject + `la` + Complement**.
