# Verbs

**Status:** Official

## Purpose

This document defines the verb system of **Luma Lina**.

The verb system is designed to be simple, predictable, and consistent. Verbs do not change according to the subject, number, or gender.

---

## Basic Verb Structure

Luma Lina uses the same verb form with all subjects.

**Structure:**

```text
Subject + Verb + Object
```

Example:

```text
Ei yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** I eat meat.

The verb `yum` remains unchanged regardless of the subject.

---

## No Verb Conjugation

Luma Lina does not conjugate verbs for:

* person
* number
* gender

The same verb form is used with singular and plural subjects.

Examples:

```text
Ei yum buff.
Ao yum buff.
Sa yum buff.
Nu yum buff.
```

These mean:

* I eat meat.
* You eat meat.
* He/she eats meat.
* It eats meat.

Plural subjects also use the same verb:

```text
Ei di yum buff.
Ao di yum buff.
Sa di yum buff.
Nu di yum buff.
```

These mean:

* We eat meat.
* You all eat meat.
* They eat meat.
* They eat those things.

The verb `yum` does not change.

---

## Verb and Negation

Negation uses the existing `na` marker.

**Structure:**

```text
Subject + na + Verb + Object
```

Example:

```text
Ei na yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** I do not eat meat.

The verb itself remains unchanged.

Plural subjects follow the same structure:

```text
Ei di na yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** We do not eat meat.

---

## Verb and Yes/No Questions

Yes/no questions use the official `ma` question marker at the end of the sentence.

**Structure:**

```text
Subject + Verb + Object + ma?
```

Example:

```text
Ei yum buff ma?
```

**Meaning:** Do I eat meat?

The verb remains unchanged.

A negative yes/no question follows the existing negation structure:

```text
Ei na yum buff ma?
```

**Meaning:** Don’t I eat meat?

---

## Information Questions

Information questions use the official `Sao` placeholder.

`Sao` replaces the missing information in its normal position.

Example:

```text
Ei yum Sao?
```

**Meaning:** What do I eat?

The verb remains unchanged.

Another example:

```text
Sao yum buff?
```

**Meaning:** Who eats meat?

---

## Tense and Aspect

Luma Lina does not currently have official tense or aspect markers.

Future development may introduce separate particles for meanings such as:

* past
* present
* future
* completed action
* ongoing action

These should be added as separate grammatical elements rather than changing the verb itself.

For example, any future tense system should preserve the basic principle that the verb itself does not conjugate.

**Status:** Not yet established.

---

## Core Rules

1. Verbs do not change according to the subject.
2. Verbs do not change according to number.
3. Verbs do not change according to gender.
4. The same verb form is used with singular and plural subjects.
5. Negation uses the existing `na` marker.
6. Yes/no questions use the existing `ma` marker.
7. Information questions use the existing `Sao` placeholder.
8. Tense and aspect are not yet officially established.
9. New verb grammar should use separate particles where possible rather than verb conjugation.

---

## Current Example

The current official verb `yum` means **eat**.

```text
Ei yum buff.
```

**I eat meat.**

```text
Ei di yum buff.
```

**We eat meat.**

```text
Ei na yum buff.
```

**I do not eat meat.**

```text
Ei yum buff ma?
```

**Do I eat meat?**

```text
Ei yum Sao?
```

**What do I eat?**

The verb remains `yum` in every sentence.
