# Possession

**Status:** Official

## Overview

Luma Lina uses the marker:

```text
de
```

`de` expresses **possession or a relationship between two words**.

It does not change according to the person, number, or gender of the possessor.

The basic structure is:

```text
Possessor + de + Possessed
```

---

## Basic Possession

The possessor comes first, followed by `de`, followed by the thing possessed.

```text
Ei de [book]
```

= my book

```text
Ao de [book]
```

= your book

```text
Sa de [book]
```

= his / her book

```text
Nu de [book]
```

= its book

The marker `de` remains unchanged.

---

## Possession with Plural Pronouns

The plural marker `di` is placed after the pronoun, before `de`.

```text
Ei di de [book]
```

= our book

```text
Ao di de [book]
```

= your all's book

```text
Sa di de [book]
```

= their book

The structure is:

```text
Possessor + di + de + Possessed
```

For example:

```text
Ei di de maimai di
```

= our goats

Structure:

```text
Ei di

we / us

de

possession / relationship

maimai di

goats
```

---

## Plural Possessed Nouns

The possessed noun can also be pluralized with `di`.

```text
Ei de maimai di
```

= my goats

Here:

```text
Ei

I / me

de

possession / relationship

maimai di

goats
```

The two grammatical markers have separate functions:

* `de` expresses possession or relationship.
* `di` expresses plurality.

---

## Possession and Pronouns

`de` can follow a pronoun to create a possessive form.

| Form       | Meaning    |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| `Ei de`    | my         |
| `Ei di de` | our        |
| `Ao de`    | your       |
| `Ao di de` | your all's |
| `Sa de`    | his / her  |
| `Sa di de` | their      |
| `Nu de`    | its        |

These forms can be followed by a noun.

Example:

```text
Ei de maimai
```

= my goat

```text
Ei di de maimai di
```

= our goats

---

## Relationship

`de` is not limited to ownership.

It can also express a relationship between two things when the meaning is clear from context.

The same structure is used:

```text
First element + de + Second element
```

The exact relationship is determined by the words and context.

This keeps possession and related relationships within one simple grammatical system rather than requiring separate markers for every relationship type.

---

## Pluralization

`de` works together with the existing plural marker `di`.

Each `di` applies to the word immediately before it.

Example:

```text
Ei di de maimai di
```

The first `di` pluralizes `Ei`:

```text
Ei di
```

= we / us

The second `di` pluralizes `maimai`:

```text
maimai di
```

= goats

Therefore:

```text
Ei di + de + maimai di
```

= our goats

---

## Core Rule

The official possession structure is:

> **Possessor + `de` + Possessed**

When the possessor is plural:

> **Possessor + `di` + `de` + Possessed**

When the possessed noun is plural:

> **Possessor + `de` + Noun + `di`**

Both can occur together:

```text
Ei di de maimai di
```

= our goats

---

## Summary

| Structure            | Meaning        |
| -------------------- | -------------- |
| `Ei de [book]`       | my book        |
| `Ao de [book]`       | your book      |
| `Sa de [book]`       | his / her book |
| `Ei di de [book]`    | our book       |
| `Ei de maimai di`    | my goats       |
| `Ei di de maimai di` | our goats      |

**Official rule:** `de` marks possession or a relationship, with the possessor placed before `de` and the possessed element after it.
