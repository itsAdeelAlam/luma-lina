# Example Sentences

**Status:** Official

## Purpose

This file contains example sentences in Luma Lina that demonstrate the current grammar and vocabulary in actual use.

Examples should use only established Luma Lina rules and vocabulary unless they are clearly marked as Proposed or Experimental.

---

## Basic Sentences

```text
Ei yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** I eat meat.

```text
Ao yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** You eat meat.

```text
Sa yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** He/she eats meat.

---

## Plural Sentences

```text
Ei di yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** We eat meat.

```text
Sa di yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** They eat meat.

---

## Copular Sentences

```text
Ei la [teacher].
```

**Meaning:** I am a teacher.

```text
Ao la [teacher].
```

**Meaning:** You are a teacher.

---

## Negation

```text
Ei na yum buff.
```

**Meaning:** I do not eat meat.

```text
Ei na la [teacher].
```

**Meaning:** I am not a teacher.

---

## Possession

```text
Ei de maimai.
```

**Meaning:** My goat.

```text
Ei di de maimai di.
```

**Meaning:** Our goats.

---

## Demonstratives


**Meaning:** this goat

```text

```

**Meaning:** these goats

```text
ta maimai
```

**Meaning:** that goat

```text

```

**Meaning:** those goats

---

## Questions

```text
Ei yum buff ma?
```

**Meaning:** Do I eat meat?

```text
Ao yum buff ma?
```

**Meaning:** Do you eat meat?

```text
Ei na yum buff ma?
```

**Meaning:** Don't I eat meat?

```text
Ei yum Sao?
```

**Meaning:** What do I eat?

```text
Sao yum buff?
```

**Meaning:** Who eats meat?

---

## Boolean Responses

```text
Ao yum buff ma?
Yah.
```

**Meaning:** Do you eat meat?

**Yes.**

```text
Ao yum buff ma?
Na.
```

**Meaning:** Do you eat meat?

**No.**

---

## Combined Examples

```text
ta maimai di la Ei di de maimai di.
```

**Meaning:** Those goats are our goats.

```text
ta maimai la Ei de maimai.
```

**Meaning:** That goat is my goat.

---

## Example Status

Examples in this file should follow the same rule-status system as the rest of the project:

* **Official** = uses accepted grammar and vocabulary.
* **Proposed** = demonstrates a suggested rule or word.
* **Experimental** = currently being tested.
* **Rejected** = intentionally discarded and retained only for documentation.

Examples should not create new grammar rules by themselves. A sentence is an example of a rule, not a rule unless the rule has been explicitly accepted.
