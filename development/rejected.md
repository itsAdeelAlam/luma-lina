# Rejected Ideas

This file records ideas that were considered and rejected during the development of Luma Lina.

Rejected ideas are not part of the current Official language.

---

## 1. `ti di maimai di`

**Rejected:** Demonstrative plural structure

**Original:**

```text
ti di maimai di
ta di maimai di
```

**Reason:** `di` was unnecessarily applied to both the demonstrative and the noun.

**Replaced by:**

```text
ti maimai di
ta maimai di
```

This follows the existing rule that `di` comes after the word it pluralizes.
